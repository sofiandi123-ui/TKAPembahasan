Reading Passage: Raja Ampat Ecosystem
Raja Ampat is an Indonesian archipelago located off the northwest tip of Bird's Head Peninsula in West Papua. The terrestrial landscape is characterized by dramatic, vegetation-covered limestone karst formations that emerge steeply from the crystal-clear turquoise waters.

Local communities work closely with international conservation organizations to manage marine protected areas and regulate sustainable ecotourism. Although its remote location presents logistical challenges, this isolation has effectively protected the fragile ecosystem from the destructive impacts of mass tourism.
